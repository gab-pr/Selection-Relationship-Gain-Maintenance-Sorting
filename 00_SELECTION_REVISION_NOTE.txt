PSYCHOLOGICAL-SELECTION CODE REVISION
=====================================

This reproducibility package uses the final wave-adjusted stable-trait / WPVS
psychological-selection workflow.

REMOVED FROM THE REPRODUCTION WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------
03_current_unpartnered_LAT_selection.R.txt
04_unpartnered_to_cohab_marriage_Cox.R.txt

Those scripts estimated earlier selection specifications based on prior
psychological means and current deviations from those means. They are not part
of the current manuscript's final selection analysis and are not included in
this package.

CURRENT SELECTION WORKFLOW
--------------------------
1. 03_prepare_current_unpartnered_selection_base.R.txt
   Creates the exact-wave clean-unpartnered landmark base only.

2. 03B_prepare_unpartnered_episode_structure.R.txt
   Creates non-overlapping clean-unpartnered episode IDs and follow-up
   boundaries only.

3. UKHLS_latent_stable_WPVS_final_measurement_stage_v2_1_WAVECENTERED_AGEFIX.R.txt
   Estimates the wave-adjusted stable-trait / WPVS decomposition and creates
   T=4 primary scores plus T=5/T=6 history-length sensitivities.

4. UKHLS_H1_latent_stable_WPVS_selection_v1.R.txt
   Fits the final H1 incident-LAT and cohabitation/marriage selection models
   using stable_score_SD and current_wpvs_SD simultaneously.

ANALYTIC PRINCIPLE
------------------
The latent measurement stage and H1 selection stage are kept unchanged from the
validated final scripts supplied for the revised manuscript. The two preceding
preparation scripts exist only to recreate intermediate objects required by
those final scripts without running the obsolete prior-mean/deviation
inferential analyses.
