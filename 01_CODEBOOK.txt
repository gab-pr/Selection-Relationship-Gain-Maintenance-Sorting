CODEBOOK AND ANALYSIS MAP
BHPS / Understanding Society relationship-course study

===============================================================================
1. DATA SOURCES AND IDENTIFIERS
===============================================================================

pidp
  Cross-wave person identifier. Used to link respondents longitudinally and as
  the respondent clustering variable in repeated-observation analyses.

survey
  Harmonized survey indicator (BHPS or UKHLS) where both survey eras contribute.

wave / wave_n
  UKHLS wave identifier / numeric wave order. Several relationship-quality and
  nonresident-partner measures are UKHLS-specific.

interview_year, interview_month, interview_ym
  Calendar timing of interview. Month indices are used for relationship duration,
  temporal ordering, and time-to-event calculations.

relationship_spell_id / lat_episode_id
  Derived identifiers for a focal cohabiting relationship spell or reconstructed
  living-apart-together (LAT) episode.

===============================================================================
2. CORE PSYCHOLOGICAL MEASURES
===============================================================================

scghq1_dv -> ghq / ghq12
  General Health Questionnaire, 12-item Likert score.
  Range: 0-36.
  Higher raw values = greater psychological distress.
  For selection models and some anticipation modules, a favorable-oriented score
  is constructed as ghq_wb = -GHQ, so higher values = lower distress.

sclfsato -> life_satisfaction / life_sat
  Overall life satisfaction.
  Range: 1-7.
  Higher values = greater life satisfaction.

STANDARDIZATION
  Standardized coefficients are expressed in outcome-specific SD units using the
  reference population specified in the relevant component script. Do not assume
  that every module uses the same SD reference. For example, the current no36
  LAT-to-cohabitation pathway deliberately reuses the incident-LAT last-unpartnered
  outcome SD so its effect scale matches the formation analysis.

===============================================================================
3. RELATIONSHIP QUALITY (RDAS)
===============================================================================

scdassat_dv -> rdas_satisfaction
  Relationship Satisfaction subscale of the Revised Dyadic Adjustment Scale
  (RDAS; Busby et al., 1995). The subscale reflects relationship stability and
  conflict (e.g., thoughts of termination, regret, quarrelling, irritation).
  Derived UKHLS score: 0-20; higher = greater relationship satisfaction.
  Available in the analysis workflow at UKHLS Waves 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.

scdascoh_dv -> rdas_cohesion
  RDAS Relationship Cohesion subscale. It reflects shared engagement and positive
  interaction, such as exchange of ideas, discussion, and shared activities.
  Higher derived scores = greater cohesion.
  Available in the analysis workflow at UKHLS Waves 1, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
  Wave 3 is not used for cohesion because a comparable derived cohesion score
  could not be constructed there.

rdas_satisfaction_change / rdas_cohesion_change
  Current RDAS value minus the most recent eligible prior value within the same
  cohabiting relationship spell. The eligible prior interval is defined in the
  core workflow (generally 6-30 months for RDAS history in these analyses).

prior_rdas_satisfaction / prior_rdas_cohesion
  Most recent eligible prior relationship-quality level within the focal spell.

===============================================================================
4. CURRENT RELATIONSHIP STATE AND LAT MEASURES
===============================================================================

mastat_dv -> harmonized status
  Current marital/co-residential status. Harmonized analysis labels include:
    single
    cohab
    married
    civil_partner
    other

ncrr1
  Steady nonresident relationship indicator among relevant UKHLS respondents.
    1 = steady relationship with a nonresident person regarded as partner
    2 = no steady nonresident partner
  Other/special codes are treated as missing.

CURRENTLY UNPARTNERED / CLEAN UNPARTNERED
  Primary exact-wave definition used in the current selection analyses:
    harmonized status == "single" AND ncrr1 == 2 at the same interview.
  Previous relationships are not an automatic primary exclusion; increasingly
  stringent prior-history exclusions are sensitivity analyses.

LAT (living apart together)
  A steady nonresident partnership. In the focal incident-LAT workflow, the
  respondent is observed single + ncrr1==2 before the event and later single +
  ncrr1==1, with reported relationship-start timing located between the relevant
  interviews under the timing rules in script 02.

ncrrm / ncrry4
  Reported month / year in which the nonresident relationship began. Used to
  derive LAT relationship-start month when available.

===============================================================================
5. SUBJECTIVE RELATIONSHIP EXPECTATIONS
===============================================================================

ncrr11
  Intention to begin living with the current nonresident partner within three
  years.
    1 = yes
    2 = no
  Script 07 recodes this as 1=yes, 0=no and defines strengthening intention as a
  no-to-yes change between eligible observations.

ncrr12
  Perceived likelihood of marrying the current nonresident partner.
    1 = very likely
    2 = likely
    3 = not sure
    4 = unlikely
    5 = very unlikely
    6 = prefer not to disclose
  Category 6 is excluded from substantive analyses. The numeric likelihood score
  is reversed so higher = stronger marriage expectation:
    original 1->5, 2->4, 3->3, 4->2, 5->1.

===============================================================================
6. PHISTORY RELATIONSHIP-HISTORY VARIABLES
===============================================================================

PHISTORY status
    2  = marriage
    3  = civil partnership
    10 = living together as a couple / cohabitation

spellno
  Relationship-history spell number.

partner
  Partner pidp when available. Used for conservative relationship continuity
  checks where the estimand requires the same focal relationship.

starty, startm / endy, endm
  Start and end year/month of the PHISTORY spell.

start_if / end_if
  Date-imputation flags used to define exact/usable timing in relevant modules.
  The preparation workflow retains the original flags and labels their status.

ongoing
  Indicator that the PHISTORY spell was ongoing at the relevant history endpoint.

cohend
  How a cohabiting spell ended. In the workflows:
    1 = breakup
    2 = transition to marriage
  Ongoing spells are treated separately.

===============================================================================
7. KEY DERIVED PSYCHOLOGICAL SELECTION VARIABLES
===============================================================================

Wave-adjusted psychological input
  Before latent decomposition, each psychological score is centered on the mean
  of its ACTUAL UKHLS survey wave in the adult UKHLS panel. For GHQ, the score is
  first favorably oriented (-GHQ-12), so higher values indicate lower distress.
  This removes common survey-wave/secular shifts before the between-/within-
  person components are constructed.

stable_score_SD
  Model-based stable between-person psychological component obtained from the
  stable-trait / within-person variability score (WPVS) measurement model. It is
  expressed in units of the outcome-specific raw current-score SD.

current_wpvs_SD
  Current within-person variability score at the focal clean-unpartnered
  baseline, conditional on the stable component. It is expressed using the same
  outcome-specific raw current-score SD as stable_score_SD.

Primary measurement history (T = 4)
  Current clean-unpartnered baseline plus the 3 immediately preceding UKHLS
  waves, all with valid psychological measurements. These are exact consecutive
  UKHLS waves. No observation after the focal baseline contributes to the score.

History-length sensitivities
  T = 5 and T = 6 repeat the decomposition with longer exact consecutive-wave
  histories.

Interpretation
  stable_score_SD and current_wpvs_SD are entered simultaneously in the final
  H1 selection models. OR/HR > 1 indicates that more favorable psychological
  functioning (higher life satisfaction or lower GHQ distress) is associated
  with greater subsequent relationship-transition odds/rate. These are
  prospective selection associations, not causal effects.

Obsolete variables
  The prior-mean plus current-deviation specification used in an earlier version
  of the selection analysis is NOT part of the current reproducibility workflow.

===============================================================================
8. INCIDENT LAT FORMATION / RELATIONSHIP-GAIN VARIABLES
===============================================================================

pre25_36
  Psychological observation approximately 25-36 months before LAT formation.

last_unpartnered
  Last eligible observation before clean incident LAT formation.

first_LAT
  First eligible observation confirming the focal steady nonresident relationship.

Primary formation contrast
  last_unpartnered -> first_LAT.
  Positive life-satisfaction change = favorable.
  Negative raw GHQ-distress change = favorable.

Primary preformation diagnostic
  pre25_36 -> last_unpartnered.

===============================================================================
9. RELATIONSHIP-AGE / MAINTENANCE VARIABLES
===============================================================================

cohab_duration_months
  Months between the start of the focal cohabiting spell and the interview.

Primary duration categories
    0-11 months   (reference)
    12-23
    24-35
    36-59
    60-83
    84-119
    120-179

spell_outcome
  Focal relationship outcome used to compare trajectories, generally:
    ongoing cohabitation
    later marriage
    breakup

Relationship-age models
  Estimate within-relationship change over duration and interactions with later
  relationship outcome. Relationship-spell and wave fixed effects are used in
  the corrected specifications. Balanced/common-spell sensitivities require the
  same spell to contribute both the 0-11-month reference and target duration bin.

===============================================================================
10. PROSPECTIVE RELATIONSHIP SORTING VARIABLES
===============================================================================

future_event
  Binary event indicator in a specified contrast and horizon.

Primary sorting horizon
  24 months after the cohabiting landmark.

Marriage contrast
  Marriage within the horizon vs observed continued cohabitation through the
  horizon. Breakups within the horizon are excluded as competing outcomes.

Breakup contrast
  Breakup within the horizon vs observed continued cohabitation through the
  horizon. Marriages within the horizon are excluded as competing outcomes.

Current-level model predictors
  Standardized current RDAS satisfaction and cohesion entered simultaneously.

Recent-change model predictors
  Standardized recent RDAS satisfaction and cohesion change, with prior RDAS
  satisfaction and cohesion levels controlled.

===============================================================================
11. PERSON-LEVEL COHABITATION / MARRIAGE SELECTION ENDPOINTS
===============================================================================

Analytic unit
  One psychologically eligible baseline per non-overlapping clean-unpartnered
  episode. A respondent may contribute again after returning to clean unpartnered
  status. Standard errors are clustered by respondent.

Cohabitation endpoint
  Cause-specific Cox model. Marriage before (or in the same recorded month as)
  observed cohabitation ends the cohabitation risk interval as a competing event.

Marriage endpoint
  First subsequent marriage during episode follow-up. Cohabitation may be an
  intermediate state and does not censor the marriage model.

Cohabitation-or-marriage endpoint
  First subsequent occurrence of either state.

Primary time scale
  Months since psychological baseline.

===============================================================================
12. ANTICIPATION / PATHWAY VARIABLES
===============================================================================

CURRENT LAT-to-cohabitation pathway (script 06)
  Among clean incident LAT episodes, a treated relationship is one that later
  progresses to a conservatively linked cohabitation at any observed time.
  A control must remain at risk in LAT through the same relationship duration at
  which the treated relationship cohabits. Psychological observations are kept
  strictly before the matched cohabitation time. Later-cohabiting controls may
  serve as controls before their own event.

  Only duration stages with adequate treated-set support enter the primary
  support-qualified omnibus. The current data support primary interpretation of
  the 12-23-month stage; later stages are too sparse for affirmative inference.

Marriage pathway (script 07)
  Compares post-cohabitation psychological trajectories of relationships that
  marry within the fixed 36-month pathway horizon with cohabitations observed to
  remain unmarried/stable through the corresponding horizon.

Subjective anticipation (script 07)
  NCRR11 change at t predicts psychological change after t.
  NCRR12 likelihood change/level at t predicts psychological change after t.
  This preserves temporal ordering of expectation and subsequent psychological
  change.

IMPORTANT
  Script 07 retains an older fixed-36-month LAT-to-cohabitation branch for
  historical continuity. It is superseded in the current manuscript by script 06.

===============================================================================
13. CLUSTERING, FIXED EFFECTS, WEIGHTING, MULTIPLICITY
===============================================================================

Clustering
  Respondent-level clustered/robust standard errors are used when repeated
  observations, repeated episodes, or matched-set reuse induce dependence.

Fixed effects
  Models use the fixed effects appropriate to their estimand, including
  relationship-spell, wave, risk-set, and/or calendar-time effects. See each
  component script for the exact formula.

Risk-set / ATT weighting
  Proximity and pathway models use the matching/weighting rules defined within
  their component workflow. The no36 LAT-to-cohabitation module uses incidence-
  density style risk-set matching at the treated relationship duration.

Multiplicity
  Holm adjustment is applied within prespecified families of related primary or
  diagnostic tests. Family definitions differ by analysis and are explicitly
  encoded in the corresponding script; p-values should not be re-adjusted across
  unrelated analysis families post hoc.

14. SCRIPT-TO-MANUSCRIPT RESULT MAP
===============================================================================

03_prepare_current_unpartnered_selection_base.R.txt
  Data preparation only: creates the exact-wave clean-unpartnered landmark base
  (status=single and NCRR1=NO at the same interview).

03B_prepare_unpartnered_episode_structure.R.txt
  Data preparation only: creates non-overlapping clean-unpartnered episode IDs
  and prospective follow-up boundaries for the Cox selection design.

UKHLS_latent_stable_WPVS_final_measurement_stage_v2_1_WAVECENTERED_AGEFIX.R.txt
  Final wave-adjusted stable-trait / WPVS measurement stage. Produces the T=4
  stable between-person and current within-person scores used by H1; T=5/T=6 are
  history-length sensitivities.

UKHLS_H1_latent_stable_WPVS_selection_v1.R.txt
  Final H1 psychological-selection analyses: matched incident-LAT conditional
  logit plus non-overlapping clean-unpartnered Cox models for cohabitation,
  marriage, and either endpoint, entering stable_score_SD and current_wpvs_SD
  simultaneously.

02_incident_LAT_formation_gain.R.txt
  Preformation and relationship-formation-associated psychological change.

01_core_relationship_development_proximity.R.txt
  Corrected relationship-age GHQ/life-satisfaction/RDAS trajectories; lagged
  RDAS-to-psychological change; primary objective proximity analyses and related
  history/weighting diagnostics.

01B_relationship_development_sensitivities.R.txt
  Balanced common-spell relationship-age sensitivities and strict nonoverlapping
  lag-ordering checks. This module underlies, among other results, the reported
  balanced RDAS satisfaction differential at 24-35 months.

01C_RDAS_gradient_pair_balanced_diagnostics.R.txt
  Additional direct RDAS proximity-gradient and pair-balanced sensitivity output.

05_RDAS_sorting_progression.R.txt
  Prospective RDAS satisfaction/cohesion levels and changes predicting marriage
  or breakup within 24 months; 12-month and weighting/history sensitivities.

06_LAT_to_cohabitation_pathway_no36.R.txt
  CURRENT cohabitation-pathway matching, support diagnostics, primary omnibus,
  balanced 12-23-month sensitivity, stricter control definitions, and first-LAT
  to last-precohabitation sensitivity.

07_pathway_subjective_anticipation.R.txt
  Marriage-pathway trajectory tests and balanced sensitivities; NCRR11
  cohabitation-intention change; NCRR12 marriage-likelihood change/level and
  behavioral-validity analysis.

15. SAMPLE-SIZE LANDMARKS FOR REPRODUCTION CHECKING
===============================================================================

Current-unpartnered source risk set
  63,158 person-wave landmarks; 18,589 respondents.

Final latent psychological selection
  Sample sizes depend on latent-score eligibility and therefore differ from the
  earlier prior-mean/deviation analyses. For exact reproduction checks, use the
  T=4 rows in:
    tables/T01_PRIMARY_T4_H1_latent_selection_coefficients.csv
    diagnostics/02_LAT_score_join_and_pruning_audit.csv
    diagnostics/05_latent_episode_baseline_and_event_support.csv
    diagnostics/06_COX_model_sample_support.csv
  T=5 and T=6 are history-length sensitivity samples and are expected to be
  progressively smaller because they require longer complete consecutive-wave
  psychological histories.

Relationship-age panels
  GHQ: 31,215 observations; 7,289 respondents; 7,486 spells.
  Life satisfaction: 31,290 observations; 7,293 respondents; 7,490 spells.
  RDAS satisfaction: 12,575 observations; 4,229 respondents; 4,271 spells.
  RDAS cohesion: 9,145 observations; 3,138 respondents; 3,156 spells.

Lagged RDAS -> psychological change
  GHQ: 4,644 observations / 1,201 spells.
  Life satisfaction: 4,682 observations / 1,207 spells.

LAT-to-cohabitation pathway
  2,736 linked clean incident LAT episodes; 318 later cohabitation progressors;
  177 retained primary matched progressor sets; 684 trajectory rows.

Marriage pathway
  1,027 future-marriage matched sets;
  19,732 life-satisfaction trajectory rows and 19,637 GHQ trajectory rows.

Subjective expectations
  Cohabitation intention: 6,241 episodes with >=2 observations; 1,586 with change.
  Marriage likelihood: 6,560 episodes with >=2 observations; 3,726 with change.

These counts are audit targets, not universal fixed Ns: fitted-model N can vary
with outcome availability, lag requirements, and sensitivity restrictions.

===============================================================================
16. INTERPRETIVE CONSTRAINTS
===============================================================================

- All analyses are observational.
- "Relationship gain" denotes formation-associated change, not a proven causal
  treatment effect of relationship formation.
- "Sorting" denotes prospective/compositional differentiation in relationship
  fate, not a causal effect of RDAS on marriage or dissolution.
- Null annual-scale anticipation results do not rule out short-lived or more
  proximal anticipatory processes that occur between survey interviews.
- The Selection-Relationship Gain-Maintenance-Sorting Model is a synthesis of
  complementary empirical contrasts, not a single estimated structural model.
